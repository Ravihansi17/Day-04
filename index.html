Nature's Beauty

Mountain

A mountain is a very high land form with steep sides that rises much higher than the land around it. Most mountains are taller than 1,000 to 2,000 feet. They often join together to form long chains called mountain ranges.

Visit About Mountains
Waterfall

A waterfall is any point in a river or stream where water flows over a vertical drop or a series of steep drops. Waterfalls also occur where meltwater drops over the edge of a tabular iceberg or ice shelf.

Visit About Waterfalls
River

A river is a natural stream of fresh water that flows on land or inside caves towards another body of water at a lower elevation, such as an ocean, lake, or another river. A river may run dry before reaching the end of its course if it runs out of water, or only flow during certain seasons.
