About Water

Lakes
About That

A lake is a large body of standing water completely surrounded by land, separate from the ocean and usually fed by rivers, streams, or precipitation.

Rivers
About That

A river is a natural stream of fresh water that flows across the land downhill. It usually starts in high places like mountains or hills from rain or melting snow, and it moves toward a lower body of water like a lake, sea, or ocean.

Oceans
About That

An ocean is a huge body of salt water that covers about 71 percent of the Earth's surface. It holds most of the water on our planet and is very deep.
